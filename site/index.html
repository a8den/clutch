FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How do you decide your offer?

We look at the device's model, storage, condition, and what it's currently selling for. Our offers are fair and simple, with no games and no lowballing after the fact unless the device doesn't match what you described.

How do I get paid?

For local meetups, we pay you in person when we meet. For mail-in orders, we pay you after the device arrives and passes inspection. We'll confirm the payment method when we send your offer.

Do you buy broken or cracked devices?

Yes. Cracked screens, bad batteries, and cosmetic damage are fine. Just tell us about it in the form so your offer is accurate.

Do you buy iCloud-locked, carrier-blacklisted, or financed devices?

No. The device must be paid off, not reported lost or stolen, and signed out of iCloud / Find My (or Google account) before sale.

What happens if the condition is different from what I described?

We'll show you what we found and send a revised offer. If you don't want to accept it, you're free to keep your device.
